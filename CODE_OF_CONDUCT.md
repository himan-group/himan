# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, behavior, and documented tradeoffs.
- Assume good intent while still asking for clear evidence and reproducible details.
- Keep discussions relevant to `himan` and its ecosystem.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or PRs; or block participants when behavior harms project collaboration.

If you need to report conduct concerns, open a minimal issue asking maintainers for a private contact path. Do not include private personal details in public.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other repository-managed collaboration spaces.
